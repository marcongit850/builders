Our shared purpose

Building a stronger future, together.

Our purpose is to encourage transparent, consistent, and predictable government processes while providing industry professionals with an organized forum to discuss concerns and recommend practical improvements.

We are more than a professional networking group. We are an advocacy organization focused on strengthening Walton County’s building industry and supporting responsible growth.

What we stand for

Our advocacy priorities

An organized voice. Constructive dialogue.
A stronger Walton County.

  1. 01

    Fair and Consistent Regulations

    Advocate for reasonable, transparent, and consistently applied building codes, zoning requirements, setbacks, and land development regulations.

  2. 02

    Permitting and Inspections

    Promote efficient, predictable, and transparent permitting and inspection processes.

  3. 03

    Private Property Rights

    Support fair treatment of private property owners and reasonable application of land development regulations.

  4. 04

    Land Development and Zoning

    Monitor proposed amendments to Walton County’s Land Development Code, Comprehensive Plan, zoning regulations, development standards, and related ordinances.

  5. 05

    Responsible Growth

    Encourage thoughtful development policies that balance economic opportunity, infrastructure capacity, environmental considerations, housing needs, and community quality of life.

  6. 06

    Infrastructure and Transportation

    Advocate for appropriate investments in roads, utilities, stormwater systems, transportation connectivity, and public infrastructure needed to support current and future development.

  7. 07

    Government Accountability

    Promote transparency, fiscal responsibility, consistency, and meaningful public participation in county government.

  8. 08

    Housing and Construction Costs

    Participate in discussions about policies affecting housing availability, construction costs, project timelines, and the ability of builders to meet community needs.

  9. 09

    Environmental and Stormwater Regulations

    Encourage clear, practical, and consistent environmental standards that protect natural resources while providing property owners and construction professionals with predictable requirements.

  10. 10

    Industry Representation

    Provide an organized forum for construction professionals to participate in discussions with the Board of County Commissioners, Planning Commission, county staff, and other government bodies.
